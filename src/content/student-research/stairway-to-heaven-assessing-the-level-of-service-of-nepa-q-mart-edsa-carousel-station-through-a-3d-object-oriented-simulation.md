abstract: |-
  Although the EDSA Carousel bus rapid transit (BRT) in Metro Manila provided an alternative mode of transportation, its makeshift stations exclusively accessible via second-story access such as footbridges raises issues on the quality of service it provides for pedestrians, as is the case with Nepa Q-Mart station. Through a 3D object-oriented simulation in SIMIO, this study used the parameters from Highway Capacity Manuals’s (HCM) level of service (LOS) to determine which parts of the Nepa Q-Mart station are prone to pedestrian congestion. A 3D model of the station and pedestrian count from a videographic survey formed the framework for the models of current rush hour conditions. Resilience of the station infrastructure is assessed by applying 10-year population growth to the present count. By analyzing these six scenarios, this study found that congestion issues persist mostly on the station stairways due to insufficient space and constricted widths, while bottleneck situations happen along the station platform. Recommended improvements for the station include widening station stairways to meet minimum standards, constructing a ramp for wheelchairs, and considering a pedestrian lane as entry points to solve stairway congestion and overall station accessibility.
