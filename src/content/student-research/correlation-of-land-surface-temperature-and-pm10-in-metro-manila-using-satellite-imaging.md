abstract: |-
  This study tackles the relationship between particulate matter 10 or PM10 and land surface temperature or LST. PM10 levels ground data were gathered by the Department of Environment and Natural Resources -- Environment Management Bureau (DENR-EMB) NCR using their sensors stationed in five (5) cities around the metro: Quezon City, Marikina, Manila, Makati, and Muntinlupa. The data are accummulative for a day taken every 6 days while the MODIS MOD11A1 LST images were downloaded from EarthExplorer. These data were then tabulated by matching the PM10 data with their respective LST data according to date and then linear regression analysis was performed per year and per season. Most of the data indicated very low correlation for the years 2015 and 2016. But when grouped according to seasons, they showed relatively higher correlation than those of in annual observations.
