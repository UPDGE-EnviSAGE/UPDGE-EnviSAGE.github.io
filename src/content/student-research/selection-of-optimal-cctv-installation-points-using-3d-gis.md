abstract: |-
  Closed Circuit Television (CCTV), or video surveillance, is an important part of modern security solutions. Despite its importance, installation of CCTV systems is done through trial-and-error, resulting to inefficiency. The objective of this paper is to examine methods through which an optimal CCTV design can be created, specifically in an outdoor area. We used a portion of the National Science Complex as our study area. Photogrammetry was used to accurately recreate the area in software. The paper proposes two methods of optimizing coverage. The first method, Non-overlapping Radius Method, is based on an algorithm that evenly distributes observers around a given area. A Python program implementing the rules of the algorithm was created that chooses where the observers will be placed in the study area. The second method presented in the study, Buffer Zone Selection Method, selects optimal points through a visibility ranking. Visibility analysis was done on all possible points of installation and an iterative algorithm was also applied in order to select the final designs. Three cases with different constraints were used to examine this method. ArcGIS is used extensively in this paper in both the selection of points and the evaluation of the coverage of the proposed optimal designs. Our results indicate the potential of these methods in the selection of optimal points for coverage analysis. Particularly, the second method presented resulted in favourable designs and results, even with no manual corrections.
