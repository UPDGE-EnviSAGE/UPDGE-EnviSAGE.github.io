abstract: |-
  Boracay Island is known for its pristine white sand beaches. In 2017, drastic algal blooms have been observed in the island which lead to the temporary 6-month closure for rehabilitation. The green color of algae comes from its green pigment which is Chlorophyll-a. Chlorophyll-a concentration is a common parameter used to measure phytoplankton biomass and nutrient status, as an index of water quality. This study aims to obtain Chlorophyll-a concentration estimates in the coasts of Boracay Island, specifically of stations 1, 2, and 3 of White Beach, Boracay, to apply Bio-optical modelling in shallow waters of coastal environments, and lastly to identify the rates of change of Chlorophyll-a concentration in stations 1, 2, and 3 of White Beach, Boracay. Bio-optical modelling is used to obtain water constituent concentrations using the Inherent Optical Properties of water such as absorption, attenuation, backscattering, remote sensing reflectance, etc. This method is a better approach in analyzing water quality since different target elements can be differentiated since they absorb and reflect differently. The study used Water Colour Simulator (WASI) to implement Bio-optical modelling in Multispectral Landsat Satellite Images to measure the Chlorophyll-a concentrations along the coasts of Boracay, specifically in stations 1, 2, and 3 of White Beach Boracay. The study area is limited to 400 meters from the coasts of stations 1, 2, and 3 of White Beach, Boracay. The satellite images used were Landsat 5 TM, Landsat 7 ETM+, and Landsat 8 OLI/TIRS to be able to obtain data for the years 1998, 2002, 2006, 2010, 2014, and 2018. The rate of change of Chlorophyll-a concentration in the three stations were identified by plotting the mean concentration values obtained. During the dry season, it was found that the rate of change in station 3 was highest followed by station 1, then station 2. During the wet season, small differences in the values of rate of change was observed but station 1 had a higher rate of change value followed by station 3, then station 2. For the entire White Beach, Boracay, the difference in rate of change was only minimal but it was higher during the wet season as compared to the dry season.
