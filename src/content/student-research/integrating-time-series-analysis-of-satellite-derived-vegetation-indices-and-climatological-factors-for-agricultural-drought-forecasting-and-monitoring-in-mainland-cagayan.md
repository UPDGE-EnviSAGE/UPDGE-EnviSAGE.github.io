abstract: |-
  The escalating global concern of agricultural drought is particularly prevalent in arid and semi-arid regions, specifically in Cagayan province which approximately 32.5% of land cover is cropland. In 2019, Cagayan experienced extreme drought amounting to Ph 2.1 billion pesos worth of agricultural crop damages. Despite its costly implications, the exploration and scientific investigation of agricultural drought have been relatively limited. Hence, forecasting and monitoring drought conditions in this area are critical in proactive agricultural drought risk mitigation. This study aims to evaluate the relationship of each vegetation indices (VIs) such as Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), Enhanced Vegetation Index (EVI), Normalized Difference Water Index (NDWI), and Normalized Difference Moisture Index (NDMI) to various climatological factors (CFs) as a measure of drought sensitivity. MODIS derived time series NDVI, EVI, NDMI and NDWI data during 2010-2021 were compared for their sensitivity to various CFs indicating drought including MODIS Land Surface Temperature (LST), CHIRPS precipitation and ERA 5 soil moisture through a series of statistical analyses. Time lag analysis was performed to understand the temporal dynamics of vegetation response to drought. The analysis showed a significant 1 cumulative lag in vegetation response to both precipitation and soil moisture, indicating that vegetation condition is influenced by the combined effects of the current and preceding monthly precipitation and soil moisture values. Results in multivariate regression revealed that NDWI (R2 = 0.76) exhibited the highest sensitivity to drought, followed closely by NDMI (R2 = 0.57). This finding underlines the potential of NDWI in detecting agricultural drought. Utilizing NDWI, a robust drought 2023 forecast with Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) of 0.039 was generated using Holt-Winters algorithm. The forecast suggests that the municipalities of Amulung, Enrile, Iguig, Piat, Solana, Tuao, and Tuguegarao City are likely to experience severe drought conditions from April to July. By highlighting these vulnerable areas, these findings in this research can serve as a basis for policymakers and local authorities to properly allocate water resources and implement appropriate mitigation measures in a timely manner.
