abstract: |-
  This study created several change detection difference maps using four vegetation indices, namely the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), the Enhanced Vegetation Index (EVI), the Normalized Difference Moisture Index (NDMI), and the Normalized Burn Ratio (NBR). The performance of the four vegetation indices for detecting the damage done in forested areas was investigated through statistical analysis. The EVI and NDVI could classify changes from vegetation to burnt areas and soil areas up to around 50-60% in the difference between its values, the NDMI displayed the most amount of changes within the area, while the NBR displayed a wider range of percent difference in values across the whole area. As observed after linearly regressing the values of each of the indices versus the validation data, the EVI had the highest coefficient for tree cover suggesting that tree cover within the area is a huge factor regarding the values of EVI making it potentially more suitable in showing forest degradation.
