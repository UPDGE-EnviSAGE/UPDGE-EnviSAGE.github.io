abstract: |-
  Infestation and diseases heavily affect crop yield and quality. Red-striped soft scale insect is unknown to infest sugarcane crops until recent reports from SRA-LAREC in 2022. This study aims to develop an innovative approach to assess the extent and severity level of red-striped soft scale infestation in sugarcane plantations in the Philippines. A spectral disease index (SDI) can identify early symptoms of infestation compared to NDVI which can only capture general plant health. The spectral signatures of normal and infested sugarcane were analyzed and the spectral bands of UAV and Sentinel-2 images affected by the disease are identified. Two SDIs designed to map infestation from these images were formulated. Results showed that the presence of red-striped soft scale heavily decreases the reflectance values of sugarcane in the red-edge and near-infrared bands for drone and Sentinel-2 images. Hence, the developed SDIs calculate the area under the curve of these bands in the spectral plot, which are given by 0.5(𝑁𝐼𝑅) + 𝑅𝐸 − 1.5(𝑅) and 0.5(𝐵𝑎𝑛𝑑 6) + 𝐵𝑎𝑛𝑑 7 + 𝐵𝑎𝑛𝑑 8 + 0.5(𝐵𝑎𝑛𝑑 8𝐴) for UAV data and Sentinel-2 image, respectively. The index should be applied to sugarcane pixels only, thus, sugarcane areas were first delineated from the images. Analysis showed that the normal sugarcane range from 1.94-3.08 for drone and 1.74-1.87 for Sentinel-2. Infested sugarcane were identified and classified into 3 levels: lightly, moderately, and severely infested. The classification was applied on 4 Sentinel-2 images to demonstrate the progression and extent of the infestation over a 2-month period.
