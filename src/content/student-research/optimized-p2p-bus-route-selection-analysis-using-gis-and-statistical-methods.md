abstract: |-
  The Point to Point Bus system (2) is a new system of transport that is being utilized in Metro Manila to case the traffic problem of the area. The P2P Bus System is designed for commuters that can afford a higher transport cost in exchange for a more convenient way of transport. The P2P Bus Systems currently existing are Trinoma-Glorieta, Megamall to SM North EDSA, Eton Centris to Glorietta and Alabang to Glorietta. In this research, a modified four-step transportation model was used to determine the transport zones where the use of a new P2P bus route may be implemented. This involves utilizing the total number of trips in a transport zone based on the trip attraction and generation data of the respective transport zones. Z-scores based on the total number of trips, average household income and car ownership percentage of data are also utilized to determine the transport zones that will optimize the use of a P2P bus terminal. Trip distribution using the gravity model is also used in finding the origin and destinations of the trips in a transport zone. Network analysis of the major roads in Metro Manila is also undertaken using traffic volume data from the MMDA to find the fastest route to be followed by the buses to and from the chosen transport zones.
