abstract: |-
  The study looks at the earthquake evacuation plan of Barangay Batasan Hills, Quezon City---how it is, at the present, a simple and straightforward plan that does not consider several factors such as road blockages and insufficient number of evacuation sites that may inhibit its objective which is to safely transport citizens to their corresponding evacuation sites in the event of an earthquake. To this matter, the research primarily makes use of various earthquake hazard data and building footprints for the creation of a vulnerability map underlying the safety of roads to be traversed in evacuating. GIS methods such as overlay analvsis in connection with Multi-criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA), and network analysis in terms of Closest Facility are carried out to accomplish a more detailed earthquake evacuation scheme. With this approach, safest and fastest routes from each block to the corresponding evacuation site were delineated. Based on the population density of the community and capacity of the only assigned shelter, an additional evacuation shelter was deemed necessary. A significant increase in the safety levels of both the shortest and fastest evacuation routes were observed after the increase of evacuation sites.
