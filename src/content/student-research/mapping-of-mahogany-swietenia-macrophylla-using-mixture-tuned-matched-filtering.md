abstract: |-
  Mahogany (Swietenia Macrophylla) is among the world's most commonly traded timber and also considered as one of the most valuable. Mahogany is classified as an exotic species in the Phiippines which is spread and planted throughout the country. Inventory and mapping of such tree species is a problem since many plantations cover large areas. To address this problem, the research aims to develop a methodology to map mahogany. Sentinel-2 image was utilized applying the partial unmixing process, Mixture Tuned Matched Filtering (MTMF). A presence and absence map of mahogany was produced with an overall accuracy of 94%. Furthermore, the MTMF score output was tested if it could accurately estimate the abundance of mahogany per Sentinel-2 pixel. an r² of 0.089 was obtained through linear regression analysis of abundance estimate and ground cover estimate. The research successfully produced a high accuracy presence and absence map but produced a poor abundance estimate.
