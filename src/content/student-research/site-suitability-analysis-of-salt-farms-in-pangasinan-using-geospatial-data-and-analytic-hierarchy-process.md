abstract: |-
  In 2020, the Philippines imported 93% (550,000 MT) of its annual salt requirement with the mere 7% being locally produced. For the past 20 years, the local salt industry has been neglected and on a steady downward trend. It is vital to increase local salt production because Filipinos consume salt on a daily basis for over 14,000 uses, ranging from being a commodity in the household, to being utilized for agricultural and industrial purposes. It is imperative, therefore, to increase the number of areas for local salt production. In this study, a suitability analysis for salt farm operations in Pangasinan was conducted using geospatial data and Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP). Physical land variables (slope, land cover, distance from coastline, distance from river, and soil texture) and meteorological factors (temperature, humidity, rainfall, wind speed) were considered to assess the suitability of an area for salt production. Results show that meteorological factors (62.5%) outweigh the physical land variables (37.5%) by a 25% margin suggesting the heavy influence of climate to salt production. The resulting suitability map was validated using existing salt farm locations in Dasol and Alaminos City using Overlap Analysis. Validation results showed 62.13% and 28.06% overlap percentages for the Dasol and Alaminos City salt farms, respectively. The main explanation for these relatively low percentage figures is because of the gaps in the output suitability map due to the slope and soil datasets. Results also showed that Bolinao, Dasol, Alaminos City, Sual, Agno, Labrador, Infanta, San Fabian, Anda, and Bani are the top 10 coastal municipalities, while Balungao, Umingan, Asingan, Mabini, Rosales, Santa Maria, Tayug, San Nicolas, Natividad, and San Quintin are the top 10 landlocked municipalities that have the most suitable locations for salt farming. The findings of this study can help policy makers, and relevant government agencies and stakeholders in planning for the construction of new salt farms in Pangasinan.
