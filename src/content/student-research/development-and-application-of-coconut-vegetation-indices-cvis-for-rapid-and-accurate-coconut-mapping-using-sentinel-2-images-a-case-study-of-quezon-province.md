abstract: |-
  The existing methods for coconut mapping in the Philippines and globally are complex, necessitating the development of a simpler yet accurate classification technique. This study introduces the first Coconut Vegetation Index (CVI) to rapidly and accurately map the coconut extent in Quezon Province. Two CVIs were developed: one for dense Coconut Vegetation (CV) and another for sparse CV. CVI_dense utilizes three Sentinel-2 bands: Near Infrared-1 (NIR-1) and Shortwave Infrared 1 and 2 (SWIR1&2), in the formula (NIR1-SWIR1)/(SWIR1-SWIR2). Meanwhile, CVI_sparse incorporates four spectral bands: NIR, Red, SWIR1, and SWIR2, in the formula (NIR1-Red)/(SWIR1-SWIR2). The formulation of these indices is mainly based on previous studies of band combinations and the mean, standard deviation of coconut’s reflectance. A coconut map of dense CV was produced using CVI_dense with a minimum threshold of 1.0940, while CVI_sparse used a threshold range of 0.4774 to 1.0940. The Balanced Accuracy metric was used to assess accuracy, accounting for the imbalanced reference data between coconut and non-coconut classes. CVI_dense proved highly effective with User’s Accuracy of 80%, Producer’s Accuracy of 88.89%, and Balanced Accuracy of 88.90%, surpassing CVI_sparse, which had accuracies of 32.00% (UA), 53.33% (PA), and 74.10% (BA).
