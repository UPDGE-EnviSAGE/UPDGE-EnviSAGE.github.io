abstract: |-
  The sugarcane industry is one of the largest industries in the Philippines. However, it suffers loss of crops worth millions due to the infestation of white grubs. White grub infestations lead to a reduced yield of 14% to 50% for sugarcane crops. With proper identification of white grub infested areas, the infestation may be controlled before it spread to different areas of the plantation. The objective of this study is to identify white grub infested areas using multispectral Sentinel-2B images. Analysis of the trend of declining NDVI and NDRE values shows potential areas that may be infested with white grubs. Supervised classification methods, specifically Maximum Likelihood, Spectral Angle Mapper and Mahalanobis Distance classification methods are utilized in this study to determine common potential areas of infestation. These potentially infested areas are further investigated through spectral analysis using an automated script created in Python. The estimated damaged areas for each month were 0%, 0.007% and 0.008% of the barangay for the months of October, December and January respectively. The study was able to identify white grub infested areas however the regions identified are only assumed to be potentially infested as the current methods could not predict, with absolute certainty, the presence of white grubs. The methods presented in the study may be used as an aid to crop monitoring to reduce the need for investigation of entire plantations as the study identifies regions that have a high probability of being infested.
