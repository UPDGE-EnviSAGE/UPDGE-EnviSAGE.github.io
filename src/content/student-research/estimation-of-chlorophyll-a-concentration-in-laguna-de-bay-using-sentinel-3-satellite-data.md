abstract: |-
  Laguna de Bay is the largest freshwater lake in the Philippines with a surface area of 911-949 km² and is situated east of Metro Manila. Its sere tinppines on is the reason why Laguna Lake has so many significant contributions to the country. Besides being a major source of fisheries to the country, Laguna Lake also supports multi-purpose uses such as a transportation route, a source of energy and also a source of irrigation. For the past years, Laguna Lake is under tremendous stress due to the industrialization around it. One of the major problems it is currently facing is the excessive amounts of nutrients present in the water, which is denoted by the chlorophyll-a concentrations. Chlorophyll-a is a type of chlorophyll present in algae and is commonly used for water quality measurement. An excessive amount of chlorophyll-a in waters may lead to lack of oxygen in the waters thus killing the aquatic life in the water. Sentinel 3 is a new satellite launched by European Space Agency (ESA) for land and ocean monitoring. It has a spectral resolution of 300 meters and contains 21 image bands. Sentinel 3 OLCI images of Laguna Lake were utilized together with established chlorophyll-a estimation algorithms from other researches and in-situ data to assess the capability of the satellite for inland waters and also to produce a map showing the chlorophyll-a concentration in Laguna Lake. A regression model was created with an R² of 0.780, an adjusted R² of 0.759 and an AIC of -18.4514. The final model shows that Sentinel 3 OLCI images can be utilized for inland water applications.
