abstract: |-
  This study will focus on the construction of 3-dimensional models of a section of a cave using low-cost photogrammetry and terrestrial laser scanning method. The results of this study may aid in the efforts of the Philippine government to assess in detail all the caves around the country. The main instruments used for this research are a Canon PowerShot SX40 HS DLSR camera and a Stone X300 Terrestrial Laser Scanner. The data was acquired at the Mystical Cave in Antipolo, Rizal. Data from the close-range photogrammetry was processed using the Agisoft Photoscan software, while data from terrestrial laser scanning method was processed using Stone Reconstructor. The accuracies of the models were assessed using distance analysis; the distances of the points on the terrestrial laser scanning model were measured and compared to the distances measured on the photogrammetric model. The distances measured from the terrestrial laser scanner were assumed to be the true value. The expected outputs for this study are the models from both methods, and the assessment of the possibility of using photogrammetry as a standalone surveying technique in cave surveying. An average error of 0.019m and a percentage error of 1.561% were obtained from the analysis.
