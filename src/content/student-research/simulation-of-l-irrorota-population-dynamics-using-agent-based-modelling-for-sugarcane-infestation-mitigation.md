abstract: |-
  Leucopholis irrorata is a common pest in the sugarcane fields of Batangas, Philippines. These pests are subterranean in nature and have the roots of the sugarcane as their primary food source, thus remedy and detection of infested crops were often too late. This study was undertaken with the objective of producing a preliminary agent-based model showing the spatiotemporal changes in a sugarcane plantation infested with white grubs. The primary data used were annual rainfall and temperature data from PAGASA, and RapidEye images of the fields from July to December both from the year 2015. The model was composed of two models: the environment model, and the white grub model. The environment model was built while considering factors such as soil moisture, soil water holding capacity, annual rainfall and temperature in the study area. The white grub model was built while considering factors such as behavior, feeding, movement, growth, and mortality. Soil moisture was computed and incorporated in the simulation to accommodate the movement of the white grubs. The study was able to use the remotely sensed images from RapidEye to produce a land classification map which served as the initial point of the simulation. Locations of potentially white grub infested areas were also determined through NDVI analysis of the images. The model produced by this study was able to show how White Grubs affect the area over a period of time. However, in its current state, the model cannot provide an accurate estimate of the damage due to white grub infestation, but may provide insights on the spatiotemporal changes during the said infestation.
