abstract: |-
  With the Sustainable Development Goal #7 (of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development of the United Nations), which spearheads the agenda's motives on affordable and clean energy, on mind, this research focuses on the application of geomatics on renewable energy, particularly solar energy. The research aims to determine the photovoltaic potential of Quezon City Hall by using Three-Dimensional Geographic Information Systems (3D GIS). The methodology consists of (1) generating a building model composed of points that contains heights derived from a LiDAR based Normalized Digital Surface Model (NDSM) and (2) determining the intersections between the building model and the sun's light rays, incorporating shadow factors and integrating solar irradiation values using 3D GIS. The research has shown that all faces of Quezon City Hall receive solar radiation and that the walls receive 3.5 times solar radiation than that of the roof. Results obtained are very promising; it will serve to encourage the development and implementation of renewable energy technologies, in this case of photovoltaic technologies. This research may help improve utilization of solar energy in the city, and become a stepping stone in achieving energy self-sufficiency and self-sustainability as a component of a smarter city.
