abstract: |-
  Recent reports state that a coral bleaching event occurred in Honda Bay, Palawan that devastated its marine ecosystem. Coral bleaching occurs when corals experience sudden environmental changes such as temperature. The main objective of this study was to determine the effect of temperature anomaly in this recent coral bleaching reports using Landsat-8 and PlanetScope Satellite images. Landsat-8 images were used to extract Sea Surface Temperature using Emissivity estimation method and validated using PAGASA Monthly Mean Surface Temperature by applying Kolmogorov-Smirnov Method and Nash-Sutcliffe efficiency. PlanetScope Images was used to produce classified benthic habitat map using Maximum Likelihood Classification and Is Data Classification was used to generate bleached and non-bleached corals. From the year 2015 to 2017 temperature dataset, an observed temperature anomaly trend occurred during months of April and May with the year 2016 as the highest anomaly. In relation to the observed trend, reports of coral bleaching were recorded on adjacent months. The result showed that from year 2016 the affected corals from bleaching were 61.47% and it increased to 70.87% after the reported bleaching on March 2018.
