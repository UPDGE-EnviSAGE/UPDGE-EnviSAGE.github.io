abstract: |-
  In order to assess the water quality of San Pablo Bay, Leyte; Chlorophyll-A estimation through regression using remote sensing approach. The in-situ point data of chlorophyll-A was taken in field last 2015 of November 9-11. During the said dates, Landsat images were not available, with this problem, the solution was to create a fusedimage that could predict and generate an image from the dates November 9-11 of 2015. To be able to generate the said data, use of Data Fusion is necessary. Data Fusion or pan-sharpening is a process which deals with data and information from multiple sources of images. Landsat 8 and MODIS were used in this thesis to achieve a refined/improved information for developing regression models for chlorophyll-a estimation. Images from Landsat 8 and MODIS websites dated before and after November 9-11, 2015 were used and predicted chlorophyll-a content at the said date. Images are dated October 3, 2015 for the before images and January 23, 2016 for the after images. The fusing of images was performed using the program ESTARFM. ESTARFM is an open source code that is readily available online and a plug-in for ENVI+IDL, this program utilizes and creates the predicted images. Using the generated image, bands information at zonal-points of in-situ data was extracted and was then used for the remote sensing regression modelling.
