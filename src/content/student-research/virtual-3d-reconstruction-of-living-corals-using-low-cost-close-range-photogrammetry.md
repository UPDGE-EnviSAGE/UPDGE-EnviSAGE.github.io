abstract: |-
  This study revolved around the utilization of low cost underwater close-range photogrammetry in demonstrating non-invasive 3D model reconstruction of living corals. The success of this study will benefit concerned researchers, especially those involved in coral monitoring. Concerned end users will be able to conduct physical observations. The researchers made use of GoPro camera, Agisoft Photoscan, and a reference object as their main materials. The general methodology was divided into three maior parts: data acquisition, data processing and accuracy assessment. Data was acquired through the sport of skin diving while Agisoft Photoscan was the tool to process the data. Lastly, accuracy was assessed by comparing the measurements obtained from the rendered 3D model with the actual dimensions of the reference cube. An average error of 0.0019m (1.9mm) was obtained from consecutive data collection and analysis. The study concluded that this methodology is reliable in conducting physical observations, which may comprise linear measurements and volume computations.
