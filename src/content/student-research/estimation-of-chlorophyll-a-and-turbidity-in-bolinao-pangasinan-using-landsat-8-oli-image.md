abstract: |-
  Assessment and monitoring of water quality of the coastal waters of Bolinao, Pangasinan is mostly limited to in situ methods only. Currently in the said area, there are two monitoring stations that continuously measure the daily changes in the coastal water quality. However, the output data of these stations are limited only to their location and immediate vicinity. To address this limitation, remote sensing products, such as satellite images, were utilized; thus making the scope of the study area more extensive. In this research, only Chlorophyll-a and Turbidity were estimated using a Landsat 8 Image. Simple regression and multiple regression analysis were applied to the acquired in-situ data and reflectance values of the satellite image to create the models for the estimation of the two water quality parameters. The resulting simple regression models yielded poor coefficients of determination or R² values when all of the in-situ data points were analyzed together. However, these values significantly improved when these points were grouped according to their relative location, mainly the aquaculture and seagrass areas. In general the R² values for turbidity estimation were highest in the aquaculture area, while the R² values for Chlorophyll-a estimation were highest in the seagrass areas. The multiple regression models also exhibited similar trends in their resulting R² (and adjusted R²) values. However these values were significantly higher compared to that of the simple regression models, hence the best models were selected from the multiple regression models. The selection of best models was based on the adjusted R² values and corrected AIC (AICc) scores of the models. Cross-validation of the best models shows that in some cases, the best models can be the ones that are simpler than the models selected using the adjusted R² criterion. Maps of the two parameters were produced using the final best models.
