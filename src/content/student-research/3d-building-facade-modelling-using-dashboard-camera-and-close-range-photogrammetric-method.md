abstract: |-
  The availability of data from car navigation system like dashboard cameras is vastly increasing. These videos when converted to images can be used with close-range photogrammetric (CRP) methods in order to generate three-dimensional (3D) models. In this study, building façades are documented using a dashboard camera on a moving vehicle and are recreated in 3D through image-based modeling. The façade of a building is considered a determining factor of the structure of a building, thus, its documentation can help in monitoring potential loss or damage. Visual clarity and relative positional measurement of its features are determined in five tests of 3D model generation. Variations in the length, number of photos, photo clarity (with or without glare), disturbances and other factors were considered in generating the 3D model. Results show that the generated 3D models of building façade have an average percent error ranging from 3.25%-10.7% with distinctive features and contains visually continuous surfaces. The results also indicate that using 400 photos in a shorter distance, at 75-meter length, generated the best 3D model with an average percent error of 3.25%.
