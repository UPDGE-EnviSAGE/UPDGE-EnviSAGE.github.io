abstract: |-
  Roads which form the primary mode of transport for passengers and goods influences the performance of a nation's economy. The Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) often conducts time-consuming and subjective-prone road condition surveys through visual and manual inspection. Use of various automated road surveying technology common in the international community, is however, rare in the Philippines due to high costing and limited available technology. Recent years have shown that the application of Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS) in transport infrastructure management, particularly in the assessment of road pavement conditions is cost-efficient and time efficient. These unmanned platforms acquire images rapidly with very high resolution and are effective in identifying and extracting the different road condition parameters (Zhang, 2009). Structure-from-Motion (SfM) is a low-cost photogrammetric technique which estimates the three-dimension (3D) structures from overlapping two-dimensional (2D) image sequences. At present, the generation of 3D point clouds are made possible through SfM software packages. This study aims to compare and conduct separate road surface 3D point clouds analysis derived from SfM software packages - Pix4D, Agisoft Photoscan, and Regard3D. Different imagery combinations were acquired using a DJI Phantom 3 Professional small unmanned aircraft system (SUAS). Each set of imagery was processed with each software deriving a 3D dense point cloud which was further analyzed for doming deformation, completeness of reconstruction, and discernibility and characterization of the pavement distress. Purely nadir imagery showed a systematic doming deformation processed with the open solutions Regard3D which were mitigated through the inclusion of tilted imagery. The proprietary solutions - Pix4D, and Agisoft Photoscan, did not show any doming deformation as this was automatically mitigated through its built-in radial distortion correction. The resulting depths of the potholes generated from each trial of each software showed millimeter level values.
