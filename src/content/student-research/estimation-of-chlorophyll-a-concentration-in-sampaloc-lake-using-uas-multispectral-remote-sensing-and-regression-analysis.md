abstract: |-
  Sampaloc Lake in San Pablo Laguna is currently providing a good source of income for the residents near the lake through fishing. An increase in the quantity of fish pens inside the lake threatens its water quality condition. One parameter being monitored at is algae biomass concentration by measuring Chlorophyll-a values. This study aims to generate a model for Chlorophyll-a Concentration for easier monitoring of Sampaloc Lake water quality and to produce a Chlorophyll-a Concentration Map of the lake. In-situ water quality data using CLW (357 points) and AAQ Water Quality Meter (12 points), with GPS coordinates, were gathered all throughout the area of the lake. Using Parrot Sequoia Multispectral Camera attached to Bee senseFly Drone, high resolution images were acquired with 80% longitudinal and lateral overlap and then processed using Pix4D. Out of 2148 images, 1496 were calibrated, producing a 18x18cm resolutionGreen (G), Red (R), Red Edge (RE) and Near Infrared (NIR) reflectance. Being the most sensitive band for identifying water features, NIR band was used to masked out the non-water features such as fish pens, land, and erroneous pixels, using Reclassification Method. The NIR reflectance map was first corrected by removing the noise using Focal Statistics and was then used to remove the sunglint of the other remaining bands; Geen, Red, and Red Edge, using Hedley's Methodology. The in-situ data and the pixel values extracted and filtered from the reflectance maps were used for Simple Linear Regression Analysis using XLStat. A model that has 5 variables of ratios R/NIR, RE², NIR², R/NIR², and NIR/RE² with an R² of 0.586 and RMSE of 0.958µ/l was generated and was used to produce a Chlorophyll-a Concentration Map of Sampaloc Lake.
