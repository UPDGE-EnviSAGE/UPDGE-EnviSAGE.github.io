abstract: |-
  With the rapid growth of population in the highly urbanized regions, solid waste management has remained a problem to solve local governments units. As for Quezon City, this problem is only bound to worsen with the recent closure of the Payatas Sanitary Landfill in December 2017 (Roxas, news.info.inquirer.net). In order to resolve this issue, new alternatives and methods on solid waste management must be employed. The study aims to present a methodological scheme for the identification and location of suitable sites for the establishment of materials recovery facilities (MRF)--to serve as intermediate facilities that conduct recycling and composting, to minimize the amount of waste output being taken to sanitary landfills. This methodological framework used Remote Sensing and Geographic Information Systems techniques for the analysis and visualization of geographical datasets. The Analytic Hierarchy Process was also utilized to determine the varying weights assigned to the site selection factors identified by the consulted experts for this study. These factors, encompassing various social, environmental, and economic considerations, are dichotomized into a final list of criteria and constraints used in the site suitability analysis. Also, a final site suitability map for MRF establishment is presented.
