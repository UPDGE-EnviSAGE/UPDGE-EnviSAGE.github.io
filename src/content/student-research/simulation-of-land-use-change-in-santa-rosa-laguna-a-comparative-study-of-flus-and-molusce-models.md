abstract: |-
  Santa Rosa, Laguna is one of the fastest growing cities in the Philippines due to its prime location, lying just 40 kilometers from Metro Manila. But despite its advances, the land use plan of the city was confined to urban areas due to its rapid growth resulting to decrease in agricultural land area to give way for residencies, commercials and industries. This study aims of to analyze land use changes and assess driving factors involved from year 2000-2015 using open-source modelling frameworks, namely, Future Land Use Change Simulation (FLUS) and Modules for Land Use Change Evaluation (MOLUSCE) models to be able to provide the local government unit of Santa Rosa, Laguna with tools effective for simulating changes that would be useful in decision making and land use planning. Both FLUS and MOLUSCE models use Artificial Neural Network (ANN) and Cellular Automata (CA). In addition to the previous objective, this study also dwelled on the differences and similarities of the two models. Lastly, predicted land use map for 2010 and 2015 were produced using raster of land use maps and seven (7) driving factors: slope, population density, proximity to roads, proximity to waterways, proximity to schools, proximity to hospitals, and proximity to roads. Land use maps produced by both models met more than 0.80 overall accuracy, hence both models are effective in future land use and land cover change (LUCC). Lastly, it was also observed that agricultural lands yielded the lowest user's accuracy after each simulation possibly due to its high number of changed pixels and absence of relevant biophysical factors such as soil types and crop suitability.
