abstract: |-
  Mangroves play an important role in the global carbon cycle. Hence, it is important to estimate carbon sequestration capacities through production of maps and estimation of parameters such as height, diameter at breast height (DBH and above ground biomass (AGB). The use of accurate species-level inventory can help in precise estimation of blue carbon and can provide information on climate change mitigation efforts as well as the total carbon stocks within the country. In this study, blue carbon stocks in two (2) mangrove sites in Banate Bay, Iloilo, Philippines was estimated from AGB calculated using remote sensing methods such as LiDAR and UAS, both of which are cost-effective and less destructive of the mangrove ecosystem. From the DSMs and DTMs from the LiDAR and UAS datasets, Canopy Height Models (CHMs) were produced. For the field data, statistical tests were used to remove outliers and 100 random samples were chosen. Using these field data points with height measurements, the RMSE of the CHIMs were calculated and the values obtained were 1.5 and 1.57 meters, for the LiDAR and UAS CHMs, respectively. The field points were also used to derive regression models for DBH using the height as the parameter and the derived equation for DBH obtained an R2 value of 0.76 and RMSE of 30.38 centimeters. Assuming that the height difference in the DBH values for four (4) years are negligible, the AGB were then calculated using published allometric equations and from these estimates, blue carbon maps were produced after conducting tree crown extraction, species identification, and applying carbon conversion factor. Based on the models, more trees were identified using the UAS compared with LiDAR datasets. A total of five (5) species were determined and Avicennia marina is found to be the most abundant species. For the blue carbon maps, it was observed that the carbon content is usually high at areas with bigger and taller trees. Given that LiDAR is an established remote sensing method, the rise of UAS gave researchers an alternative method which is cheaper and convenient without sacrificing accuracy.
