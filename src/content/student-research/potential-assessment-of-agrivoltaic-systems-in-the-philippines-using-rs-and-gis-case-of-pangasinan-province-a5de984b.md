abstract: |-
  The Philippines is facing problems regarding its inadequate supply of energy and food insecurity. Agrivoltaic farming has the potential to solve both of these problems at the same time by incorporating solar panels in rice farming areas. This study aims to conduct an assessment of the suitability of agrivoltaic farms in Pangasinan using GIS with OPA-MCDM analysis. Estimated theoretical solar PV output for different solar panel products, in terms of peak power, is computed for areas deemed suitable for agrivoltaics. The crop yield computation was done using data obtained from PhilRice. The profitability of solar farms, agrivoltaic farms, and traditional agriculture farms was evaluated using a cost-benefit analysis (CBA) to determine the best choice of farm. Data analysis showed the estimated solar yield of solar farms is greater than the agrivoltaic farm. The traditional agriculture farm was able to produce more crop yield than the agrivoltaic farm. The solar farm was able to generate Php 8,734,500,000.00, the agrivoltaic farm produced Php 5,259,000,000.00, and the traditional agriculture farm produced Php 3,687,900,000.00 of profit. Urdaneta City and Asingan ranked in the top ten both in terms of electricity and crop yield.
