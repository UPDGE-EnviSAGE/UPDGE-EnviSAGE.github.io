abstract: |-
  Characterization of shallow waters is vital in sustaining and maintaining ecosystems under water, yet it is one of the constraints of conventional echo sounding methods which used large and heavy vessels, which harms both itself and the ecosystems involved. The aim of this research is to test an alternative way to address this problem through the use of a customized Unmanned Surface Vehicle that is equipped with a Global Navigation Satellite System receiver, echo sounder, video recording device, and motion corrector. The depth of different seabed types was determined using and echo sounder and image aquisition, which was then verified using points from a bathymetric survey. The extracted data was used to produce a 3D model and bathymetric map that consists of the different seabed types that shows the characteristics of the shallow waters with the average residual of the SONAR surface and photogrammetry surface being approximately 0.164 meters.
