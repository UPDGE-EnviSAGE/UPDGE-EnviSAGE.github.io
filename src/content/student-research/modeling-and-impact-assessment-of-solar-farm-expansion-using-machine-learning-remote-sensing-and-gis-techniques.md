abstract: |-
  The burning of fossil fuels for electricity generation significantly contributes to global carbon emissions and climate change. In the Philippines, the expansion of ground-mounted solar PV farms could maximize generation capacity. However, there are insufficient studies on modeling such expansions. As such, this study aims to create a predictive model for solar farm expansion in Tarlac Province, Philippines, using remote sensing (RS), Geographic Information System (GIS), and machine learning techniques. Inputs to the MOLUSCE plugin in QGIS include satellite-derived LULC datasets from NAMRIA, classified Landsat 8 images, MODIS land cover product (MCD12Q1.061), and spatial variables like slope, shortwave radiation, nighttime lights, population density, and proximity to roads, electrical lines, and substations. Among the spatial variables, proximity to electrical lines and substations showed the highest correlation, at 0.939. Transition potential modeling (TPM) methods such as artificial neural networks (ANN), weights of evidence (WoE), and logistic regression (LR) were used to predict LULC change through CA-Markov simulation. Kappa validation for 2025 LULC yielded values of 0.97 to 0.99 for NAMRIA, 0.81 to 0.88 for MODIS, and 0.74 to 0.78 for Landsat 8, which indicates that all models are suitable to forecast the 2040 LULC. Subsequently, the model using the NAMRIA LULC dataset and WoE TPM with a 15-interval set achieved the highest F1 score of 0.772 and Intersection over Union (IoU) of 0.628, making it the most optimal in modeling solar farm expansion. From 2015 to 2025, 133.2 hectares of croplands transitioned to solar farms which led to an estimated agricultural loss of Php 59,956,836.48, while the 1.98-hectare transition of croplands to solar farms from 2025 to 2040 equates to a Php 891,250.27 agricultural loss. The expansion is also expected to generate 27,633.26 MWh of electricity annually from 2015 to 2025, with an additional 877.75 MWh annually from 2025 to 2040. Results also showed that by 2040, solar farm expansion in Tarlac Province can reduce carbon emissions by 24,105.28 tCO2e. Lastly, refining the model with more suitable spatial variables and exploring other machine learning parameters is recommended. The results of this study can guide solar energy companies and the country’s energy sector on the trajectory and scale of solar farm expansion.
