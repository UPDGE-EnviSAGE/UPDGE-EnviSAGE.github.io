abstract: |-
  Watershed is the region of land that contributes water to a stream, river, pond, lake, or other body of water. The assessment of material transport is crucial for assessing watershed health. Laguna de Bay, the largest lake in the Philippines, supports the aquaculture industry, hydro-power generation, water supply, and transportation of people and goods between communities. Determination of the significant factors affecting the sediment discharge can provide how their impacts can be mitigated and can be used to aid local government units in decision-making and urban planning. GSSHA model was used to generate as sediment concentration during a significant rainfall event because of its capability in short term sediment transport simulations and its 2D-overland flow computations. The study was able to generate a hydrologic discharge and sediment concentration model with NSE = 0.87 and R² = 0.88. On class level, spatial patterns metrics exhibit fragmentation information using the landscape mosaic of patches about the landscape ecology. The proponents were able to develop class level spatial pattern metrics - based regression model for the estimation of sediment concentration of the respective study area. Based on regression, with the coefficient of determination R² = 0.995 and RMSE = 0.378, there is an observed significant relationship of sediment concentration to the land cover class level spatial pattern metrics. Significant correlations have been found between the sediment concentration and the built-up and crops of the generated scenarios. Integrating landscape spatial pattern metrics analysis can help improve understanding landscape spatial pattern ecology either being influenced by natural or man-made activities to each other. Moreover, it can also be utilized to incorporate a perspective into policies and guidelines for managing public domains.
