abstract: |-
  Car parking is one of the biggest problems in transport management in most highly urbanized cities where the supply does not meet the demand for parking spaces not only because of the lack thereof of supply but also because of the inefficiency of urban planning by almost always disregarding parking matters in the same. In Quezon City, known to be the biggest and most populous city in the Metropolitan Manila and house to many different institutional, commercial, and residential land use areas, the growing issue with regards to parking has been apparent over the years. The high-density traffic congestion caused by high level of vehicular movement (vehicle inflow-outflow in the area) and the demand in the usage of the different land uses aforementioned in addition give rise to a serious problem in parking. In Quezon City, one of the prospect area as the future center of business is the proposed Quezon City Central Business District (CBD), also known as the North-East Triangle Park. Here, commercial and residential projects would most likely to be developed, and when there is development, there is a rising need to consider and assess parking matters and the effects such developments could bring to the same. Thus, in this study, assessment of the parking patterns in the CBD was performed twice, one for the existing current land use/development, and the other considering imminent developments in the area. Results show how different land use and type of developments in an area affect parking patterns. In QC-CBD, there still are many buildings that have insufficient parking supply. With these results, optimal locations for new parking garages were determined using GIS-based methodologies. Despite determining optimal locations for these parking garages, results show how there would still be insufficient and inefficient.
