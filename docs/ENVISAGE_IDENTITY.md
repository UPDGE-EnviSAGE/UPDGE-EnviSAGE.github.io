# EnviSAGE Identity Reference

Status: Internal identity and communication reference

This document records the current working identity guidance for EnviSAGE. It is not a formally adopted founding charter and should not be described publicly as one.

## Official Name

Environmental Systems Applications of Geomatics Engineering (EnviSAGE)

## Public Description

EnviSAGE is the environmental geomatics research laboratory of the UP Department of Geodetic Engineering.

## Tagline

Spatial Understanding for Environmental Sustainability

## Acronym Meaning

SAGE means Systems Applications of Geomatics Engineering.

## Institutional Context

EnviSAGE should be presented through this affiliation hierarchy:

1. University of the Philippines Diliman
2. College of Engineering
3. Department of Geodetic Engineering
4. EnviSAGE

Public language should clearly acknowledge UP and the UP Department of Geodetic Engineering while keeping EnviSAGE framed as a research laboratory, not as a separate UP unit.

## Purpose

EnviSAGE connects the technical strength of Geomatics Engineering with environmental research questions. Its work uses Earth observation, GIS, spatial analytics, environmental modeling, geospatial data science, and related spatial technologies to understand environmental systems and support evidence-based decisions.

## Mission Emphasis

Public mission copy should prioritize:

1. Research for environmental sustainability
2. Education and student mentorship
3. Training, extension, and collaboration

## Research Philosophy

Environmental challenges are spatial. They unfold across places, scales, and time, shaped by linked conditions across land, water, atmosphere, ecosystems, infrastructure, and society.

EnviSAGE approaches environmental questions through systems thinking and spatial evidence. Maps, satellite observations, field measurements, model outputs, and spatial analyses are useful when they help explain relationships and support responsible environmental decisions.

## Public Tone

Public pages should be concise, confident, and accessible. They should explain what EnviSAGE is, what it studies, and how people can explore its work without exposing internal governance, content-model, maintainer, or review terminology.
