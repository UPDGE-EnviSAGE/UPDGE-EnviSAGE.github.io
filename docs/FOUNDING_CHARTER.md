# Founding Charter

## Environmental Systems Applications of Geomatics Engineering (EnviSAGE)

University of the Philippines  
Department of Geodetic Engineering

**Spatial Understanding for Environmental Sustainability**

## 1. Preamble

Environmental challenges today are complex, interconnected, and increasingly spatial. Changes in land, water, atmosphere, ecosystems, infrastructure, and society do not occur in isolation. They unfold across places, scales, and time, shaped by natural processes and human decisions.

Geomatics Engineering offers a rigorous way to observe, measure, analyze, and interpret these relationships. Through positioning, remote sensing, mapping, spatial data science, and geographic reasoning, it helps reveal patterns that are difficult to understand from isolated observations alone.

Environmental Systems Applications of Geomatics Engineering (EnviSAGE) was established as the research laboratory of the UP Department of Geodetic Engineering to bring this spatial understanding to environmental questions. The laboratory exists to connect the technical strength of Geomatics Engineering with the scientific and societal need to understand environmental systems more clearly.

## 2. Our Purpose

EnviSAGE exists to integrate Geomatics Engineering, Earth observation, spatial analysis, environmental science, and emerging geospatial technologies in the study of environmental systems.

The laboratory seeks to produce knowledge, methods, tools, and people capable of supporting society in understanding environmental conditions, monitoring change, and making evidence-based decisions. Its work is grounded in the belief that environmental sustainability requires both scientific understanding and the ability to translate that understanding into spatial information that can be used responsibly.

## 3. Vision

EnviSAGE envisions a society that understands environmental systems through space, evidence, and shared responsibility.

The laboratory aspires to contribute research, education, and geospatial methods that help communities, institutions, and decision-makers see environmental change with greater clarity, act with stronger evidence, and steward natural systems with care.

## 4. Mission

The mission of EnviSAGE is to advance environmental sustainability through research, education, innovation, and collaboration in Geomatics Engineering and geospatial science.

EnviSAGE commits to:

- Generate scientific understanding of environmental systems through spatial observation, analysis, and modeling.
- Develop evidence-based decision support for environmental monitoring, planning, and management.
- Educate students and researchers who are technically competent, scientifically rigorous, ethical, collaborative, and socially responsible.
- Innovate with geospatial technologies while keeping environmental questions at the center of the work.
- Collaborate with universities, government, industry, communities, and international partners without losing sight of public service and scientific integrity.

## 5. Our Philosophy

Understanding environmental systems requires integrating observations across land, water, atmosphere, and society.

EnviSAGE approaches environmental questions through this integrated spatial lens. A satellite image, field measurement, model output, map layer, community observation, or statistical result is most useful when placed in relation to other evidence. The laboratory therefore treats spatial data not as an end in itself, but as a way to connect processes, places, decisions, and consequences.

This philosophy recognizes that environmental systems are dynamic. Coastlines shift, waters change, habitats respond, cities expand, hazards interact with exposure, and communities experience environmental change in uneven ways. EnviSAGE seeks to understand these relationships with scientific care and spatial clarity.

## 6. What Makes EnviSAGE Different

The world does not need another GIS laboratory if GIS is understood only as software, map production, or technical service.

EnviSAGE is founded on a broader idea. It studies environmental systems through a spatial lens. Remote sensing, GIS, Earth observation, geospatial AI, models, field observations, and spatial analytics are important, but they are tools in service of environmental understanding.

The laboratory's identity is therefore not defined by any single platform, dataset, algorithm, or technology. It is defined by the questions it asks: how environmental systems are changing, how those changes can be observed, how spatial evidence can be made reliable, and how that evidence can support society.

## 7. Scientific Approach

EnviSAGE integrates remote sensing, GIS, Earth observation, field observations, geospatial AI, spatial analysis, environmental monitoring, environmental modeling, and quantitative methods.

These approaches are used to observe environmental conditions, detect change, classify and characterize landscapes and ecosystems, assess spatial patterns, evaluate uncertainty, and communicate results. The laboratory values methods that are transparent, reproducible, scientifically grounded, and appropriate to the environmental question being studied.

Technology will continue to evolve. EnviSAGE welcomes new tools when they improve scientific understanding, but it does not treat novelty as a substitute for rigor.

## 8. Core Values

### Scientific Integrity

EnviSAGE values careful methods, honest interpretation, transparent assumptions, and respect for uncertainty. The laboratory seeks to produce research that can be examined, questioned, reproduced where possible, and improved.

### Curiosity

Environmental systems are complex and often resistant to simple explanations. EnviSAGE encourages curiosity that asks better questions, explores relationships across scales, and remains open to what evidence reveals.

### Innovation

Innovation in EnviSAGE is purposeful. The laboratory develops and applies new geospatial methods, models, workflows, and tools when they help deepen environmental understanding or improve decision support.

### Collaboration

Environmental problems cross disciplinary and institutional boundaries. EnviSAGE values collaboration among researchers, students, communities, agencies, industry, and partners who bring different forms of expertise and responsibility.

### Environmental Stewardship

The laboratory's work is guided by care for environmental systems and the people who depend on them. EnviSAGE seeks to produce spatial knowledge that supports responsible stewardship of land, water, coastal, marine, and atmospheric environments.

### Service to Society

As a laboratory within the University of the Philippines, EnviSAGE recognizes public service as part of its academic responsibility. Its research and training should help society understand environmental change and act with better evidence.

## 9. Research Themes

EnviSAGE's current research themes are:

1. Coastal & Marine Systems
2. Ecosystems, Biodiversity & Land Change
3. Water, Air & Environmental Quality
4. Climate, Hazards & Resilience
5. Urban & Sustainable Systems

These themes describe the laboratory's present environmental systems and problem domains. Cross-cutting Geomatics Approaches are defined in `docs/RESEARCH_TAXONOMY.md`. The themes may evolve as environmental challenges, scientific questions, and geospatial methods evolve.

## 10. Education

EnviSAGE is committed to developing graduates and researchers who are technically competent, scientifically rigorous, environmentally responsible, socially responsible, ethical, and collaborative.

The laboratory views education as more than technical training. Students should learn to ask sound research questions, evaluate evidence, document methods, communicate results, work with others, and understand the societal and environmental consequences of geospatial work.

## 11. Collaboration

EnviSAGE welcomes collaboration with universities, government agencies, industry, communities, and international partners where shared work can advance environmental understanding and public value.

Such collaboration should be built on scientific integrity, mutual respect, clear roles, responsible data use, and careful communication of results. The laboratory does not claim partnerships that have not been established, but it recognizes collaboration as essential to meaningful environmental research.

## 12. Geographic Perspective

EnviSAGE's primary geographic focus is the Philippines.

The country presents urgent and varied environmental questions across coasts, watersheds, islands, cities, agricultural landscapes, forests, reefs, seagrass beds, mangroves, hazards, and climate-sensitive communities. These settings make the Philippines a vital context for geospatial environmental research.

At the same time, environmental systems do not stop at national boundaries. EnviSAGE remains open to regional and global collaboration, especially where shared methods, comparative studies, and international research can strengthen work in the Philippines and contribute to broader scientific understanding.

## 13. Looking Forward

Technologies will evolve. Environmental systems will change. The questions facing society will become more complex.

EnviSAGE's mission remains to bring spatial understanding to environmental sustainability: to observe carefully, analyze rigorously, collaborate responsibly, educate well, and help society act with clearer evidence.
